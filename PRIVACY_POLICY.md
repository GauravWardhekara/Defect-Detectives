## 1. Information We Collect
We may collect information about you in a variety of ways depending on how you interact with the Application. The information we may collect via the Application includes:

- **Personal Data:** If you authenticate using our Microsoft Azure Active Directory (MSAL) integration, we may collect identifiable information such as your name, email address, and profile information provided by your identity provider.
- **Device Data:** We may collect device information (such as your mobile device ID, model, and manufacturer), operating system, version information, and IP address.
- **Camera and Media Data:** The Application allows you to capture photos and videos to document defects. We only access your device's camera and photo library with your explicit permission.
- **Application Data:** Data you enter into the Application regarding defects, tasks, or related notes is stored to provide the core functionality of the Application.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we may use information collected about you via the Application to:
- Create and manage your account.
- Provide the core functionality of tracking and managing defects.
- Process data through integrated AI services (e.g., Google GenAI) to assist in defect analysis and reporting.

- Respond to your inquiries and offer customer support.
